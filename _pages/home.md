<section class="intro-band">
  <div>
    <p class="eyebrow">Our approach</p>
    <h2>From molecular interactions to systems-level insight</h2>
  </div>
  <p>Immune cells must respond precisely to pathogens and signaling molecules. We study the signal-activated and cell-state transcription factors that coordinate these responses, linking biophysical mechanisms to genome-scale behavior in healthy and disease states.</p>
</section>

<section class="home-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">Research themes</p>
    <h2>How we investigate immune gene regulation</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-grid">
    <article class="feature-card">
      <span class="feature-card__number">01</span>
      <h3>TF specificity</h3>
      <p>We examine how closely related interferon regulatory factors and nuclear receptors recognize DNA and regulate distinct target genes.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card">
      <span class="feature-card__number">02</span>
      <h3>Immune cell state</h3>
      <p>We study how lineage and polarization factors shape enhancer landscapes and cell-specific responses in monocytes and macrophages.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card">
      <span class="feature-card__number">03</span>
      <h3>Technology development</h3>
      <p>We develop protein-binding microarray and functional genomic approaches to characterize TF binding, cooperativity, and allostery.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
